4. How to Enter

No purchase is necessary to enter, and there is no cost to submit. To enter, an authorized representative of the Entrant's Organization must complete the official submission form in full, including:

  • Entrant and organization contact information;
  • Names of all individuals and companies involved in the submitted campaign;
  • Campaign details, including flight dates and a link or upload of the creative;
  • Responses to the goals, performance, and “behind the scenes” questions on the form; and
  • Acknowledgment of these Terms and the licenses granted in Section 10.

An Entrant's Organization may submit more than one campaign, and a single campaign may be submitted to more than one eligible category, provided a separate, complete entry form is submitted for each category. Incomplete entries may be disqualified at Sponsor's discretion.

Industry Categories will be awarded only if that Category receives two (2) or more qualifying entries. Sponsor reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to add, remove, combine, rename, or decline to award any Category, including for insufficient entries, before winners are announced.

6. Judging & Selection

Eligible entries will be reviewed by a panel comprised of Spaceback team members and independent external judges (the “Judging Panel”). Entries will be evaluated based on criteria that may include, without limitation:

  • Creative quality and execution;
  • Campaign performance, supported by the quantitative and qualitative data provided;
  • Strategic approach and goal alignment;
  • Innovative or effective use of Spaceback's ad formats and AI-powered features; and
  • Overall impact, as described in the submission.

Category weighting and methodology are determined by Sponsor and the Judging Panel in their sole discretion. Not every Category is guaranteed to have a winner. All judging decisions are final and not subject to appeal.
